# MVP — Minimum Bar

Full grading detail (what separates each level) lives in `rubric.md`. This
is the bar:

- [ ] **All 3 required variables profiled** (`mass (g)` given as the worked
  example, plus `year` and `reclat`) — five-number summary run, shape
  correctly labeled symmetric/skewed, and a real outlier (or explained
  non-outlier oddity) identified for each.
- [ ] **The AI-mistake exercise done** — a specific, correct explanation
  of what the given AI interpretation gets wrong, backed by your own
  five-number summary/histogram.
- [ ] **One non-obvious pattern or segment mined**, with the mean-or-median
  choice justified by that variable's actual skew.
- [ ] **The standard-deviation question answered**, tied to `mass (g)`'s
  real numbers, connected to what a large spread implies about a
  real-world/business metric.
- [ ] **`starter/memo.md` complete** — at least 2 specific, checkable
  clarifying questions about the data's origin, completeness, or bias, plus
  a paragraph connecting at least one to whether you'd trust your mined
  pattern.
- [ ] **Clean notebook** — runs top to bottom without errors, no leftover
  debugging cells.
- [ ] **GitHub repo** — public, at least 3 real, well-messaged commits.

See `CHECKLIST_TIMELINE.md` for the day-by-day sequence this bar is built in.
