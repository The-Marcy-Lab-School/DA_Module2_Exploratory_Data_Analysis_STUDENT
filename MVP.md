# MVP — Minimum Bar

Full grading detail (what separates each level) lives in `rubric.md`. This
is the bar:

- [ ] **Stakeholder chosen** (`SCENARIOS.md`), their question restated in
  your own words.
- [ ] **Real pandas exploration** of the variable(s) your question needs —
  written by you, not filled into a given template; at least one grouped
  summary and one sort/filter.
- [ ] **The same core analysis redone in Polars**, with correct real
  syntax (not a copy-pasted pandas line with the library name swapped).
- [ ] **A real, run-twice timing comparison**, with all 4 tradeoffs
  questions answered — including what changed on Polars' second run and
  why.
- [ ] **The AI-vision exercise done for real** — your own chart, saved as
  an actual image, a real AI's verbatim response to it, and your own
  critique of that response against your five-number summary.
- [ ] **Your stakeholder's question answered directly**, backed by the
  statistic (mean or median) that fits your variable's actual skew.
- [ ] **The standard-deviation question answered**, tied to `mass (g)`'s
  real numbers, connected to what a large spread implies about a
  real-world/business metric.
- [ ] **`starter/memo.md` complete** — your stakeholder and their question
  named, your answer restated for a non-technical reader, at least 2
  specific, checkable clarifying questions about the data's origin,
  completeness, or bias, plus a paragraph connecting at least one to
  whether your stakeholder should trust your answer.
- [ ] **Clean notebook** — runs top to bottom without errors, no leftover
  debugging cells.
- [ ] **GitHub repo** — public, at least 4 real, well-messaged commits.

See `CHECKLIST_TIMELINE.md` for the day-by-day sequence this bar is built in.
